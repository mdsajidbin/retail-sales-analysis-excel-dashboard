# Retail Sales Analysis & Interactive Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data from 2023 to 2025 using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, compare regional and product performance, and generate actionable business insights through PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive Slicers
- KPI Dashboard Design
- Sales and Profitability Analysis
- Business Insights and Data Visualization

## Key Analysis
- Annual sales and profit performance
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Regional sales and profitability comparison
- Product category and sub-category performance
- Sales channel and customer segment analysis
- Discount impact on profit margins
- State-level profitability analysis
- Top customer sales contribution

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for Net Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Units Sold, Total Orders, and Average Order Value (AOV)
- Interactive filters for Year, Region, Category, Sales Channel, and Customer Segment
- Visual analysis using charts and PivotTables
- Business findings and recommendations

## Key Objectives
- Understand what drives sales and profitability.
- Identify products and regions with strong or weak performance.
- Evaluate discount patterns and their relationship with profit margins.
- Present findings in a clear and interactive executive dashboard.

## Dataset
The project uses a synthetic retail sales dataset for learning and portfolio demonstration. It does not represent actual confidential company data.

## Outcome
Developed an Excel-based sales analytics project that demonstrates data analysis, business reporting, dashboard design, and data-driven decision-making skills.
